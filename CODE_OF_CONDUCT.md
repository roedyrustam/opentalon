# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge / Komitmen Kami

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **OpenTalon** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, welcoming, and technically constructive experience for everyone—regardless of background, experience level, nationality, or preferred language (English, Bahasa Indonesia, or others).

## Our Standards / Standar Komunitas

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and technical rigor toward other contributors.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code review feedback.
- Writing clean, complete, well-tested code adhering to OpenTalon's Zero-Slop engineering standards.
- Helping newcomers get started with `good first issue` tasks, `SKILL.md` authoring, and LLM driver integrations.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information or API credentials without explicit permission.
- Submitting spam pull requests or malicious code targeting the sandbox or supply chain.

## Enforcement / Penegakan

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
